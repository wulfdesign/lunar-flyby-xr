## 🚀 Why We Evolved Our License

*Artemis: The Free Return* began as an agile prototype exploring real-time Newtonian orbital mechanics in WebXR. As the simulation has matured into a mathematically rigorous, festival-selected cislunar flight engine with upcoming modular mission packs, high-resolution 3D spacecraft assets, and museum exhibition interest, we transitioned our licensing model to **Source-Visible / Proprietary with Free Public Web Play**.

Here is why this change was made and what it means for the community:

### 1. 🌐 The Free Return Remains 100% Free
The flagship simulation at `wulfdesign.github.io/lunar-flyby-xr/` is and will **always remain 100% free** to play in any modern web browser and WebXR headset (Meta Quest, PCVR, Apple Vision Pro). There are no accounts, no paywalls, and no microtransactions for the core cislunar free-return mission.

### 2. 🔍 Academic Openness & Transparent Source
The complete source code remains publicly accessible on GitHub. Students, educators, aerospace engineers, and indie developers are encouraged to inspect our custom Velocity Verlet numerical integrator, Vis-viva TLI targeting solver, and continuous Hermite time-warp algorithms for personal learning, educational study, and scientific peer review.

### 3. 🛡️ High-Fidelity 3D Models & Asset Protection
As we integrate high-resolution 3D models of the Orion spacecraft, European Service Module, Space Launch System (SLS), and Lunar Gateway, these digital assets involve specialized 3D artist licenses and NASA media guidelines. Standard permissive open-source licenses (such as MIT) permit unrestricted extraction and commercial resale of embedded files, which violates upstream 3D artist agreements. Our updated license protects these creative assets from unauthorized extraction and resale.

### 4. 🏛️ Museum, Science Center & Exhibition Partnerships
While individual classroom teachers are warmly welcomed to project the public web simulation onto classroom screens for educational lectures at zero cost, **ticketed public venues (science centers, museums, planetariums, and commercial festivals)** charge admission to hundreds of thousands of guests.  
To support these venues properly, we offer formal **Institutional Exhibition Licenses** featuring turnkey kiosk configurations, custom branding, high-framerate PCVR optimization, and curator support.

### 5. 🧑‍🚀 Sustainable Indie Craftsmanship
Engineering real Newtonian N-body physics and aerodynamic lifting reentry in vanilla Three.js requires thousands of hours of specialized craft. Retaining proprietary rights prevents predatory bot-farms and clone studios from packaging our work into unauthorized paid app-store clones, while enabling our studio to sustainably build upcoming historical mission expansions (Apollo 11, Mars Aerobraking, Lunar Gateway Rendezvous).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Can I play the simulation for free?**  
  **Yes!** Simply visit [`wulfdesign.github.io/lunar-flyby-xr/`](https://wulfdesign.github.io/lunar-flyby-xr/) in your browser or VR headset.
* **Can I view the source code on GitHub?**  
  **Yes.** The source code is publicly visible on GitHub for educational study and scientific peer review.
* **Can I use this in my K-12 or university classroom?**  
  **Yes.** Teachers and professors may project the live web experience for classroom lectures and student demonstration free of charge.
* **Can I install this on a museum, planetarium, or festival kiosk?**  
  Public, unattended kiosk installations and ticketed venue exhibitions require a formal **Institutional Exhibition License**. Please contact `wulfdesignstudios@gmail.com` to partner with us!
* **Can I repackage this into a paid app on Steam, itch.io, or Meta Quest?**  
  **No.** Commercial distribution, standalone packaging, and resale are strictly reserved by Wulf Design Studios.
